## v1
- Describe a part and get an editable 3D design with named sizes (width, wall, hole diameter…), plus STEP, STL and 3MF files.
- Resize or tweak a design by name ("make it 80 wide", "wall 2.5 mm") and see the size and volume before and after.
- Open STEP, IGES, STL, 3MF and OBJ files to get their size, volume, surface area and centre of mass, and the design history of editable designs.
- Measure hole diameters, hole spacing, wall thickness and the angle between faces exactly.
- Render pictures of a part from standard views (isometric, front, top, sides).
- Export to STEP, IGES, STL, 3MF or OBJ, with a print-readiness check: closed mesh and volume matching the exact solid.
- Look up every modelling command and its options before writing build steps.
